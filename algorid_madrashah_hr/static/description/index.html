Madrashah Management System - Staff & Teacher Information System

Odoo 19 Community Teacher Profiles Schedule Management

📋 Overview

Madrashah HR & Teacher Management extends Odoo's HR module to provide comprehensive teacher management for Islamic educational institutions. Manage teacher profiles, qualifications, class assignments, and work schedules.

🔗 Integrated with Odoo HR: Leverages Odoo's built-in HR module, adding education-specific features for teachers and academic staff.

✨ Key Features

  • 👤 Teacher Profiles

    Extended employee profiles with teacher-specific information: qualifications, specialization, experience.

  • 📚 Subject Assignment

    Assign teachers to multiple subjects they can teach with expertise levels.

  • 🏫 Class Assignment

    Assign teachers to classes and sections. Define class teachers for each section.

  • 🎓 Qualification Records

    Store educational qualifications: degrees, certifications, institutions, years.

  • 📅 Work Schedule

    Define weekly work schedules for teachers with time slots and room assignments.

  • 📊 Experience Tracking

    Track total teaching experience, previous employment, and tenure at current institution.

📑 Teacher Information Captured

Category Fields
Personal Name (English & Bangla), Date of Birth, Gender, Religion, Blood Group
Professional Teacher ID, Joining Date, Experience Years, Specialization
Academic Highest Qualification, Subject Expertise, Classes Assigned
Schedule Weekly Schedule, Periods, Rooms, Break Times
